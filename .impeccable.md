## Design Context

### Users
People memorizing scripture and other spoken material through focused, repeatable training exercises.

### Brand Personality
Calm, thoughtful, and encouraging. The experience should support concentration and confidence without feeling clinical or overstimulating.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial parchment aesthetic using the app’s established navy, brass, amber, and green palette. Retain the existing light theme, serif display typography, warm surfaces, and restrained visual character.

### Design Principles

- Preserve focus: use color to clarify hierarchy and state, not as decoration.
- Keep the established parchment and ink visual language consistent.
- Use navy for structure, brass and amber for primary/default emphasis, and green for healing or successful states.
- Pair color with labels, shape, or symbols so meaning never depends on color alone.
- Favor progressive disclosure and compact controls over persistent visual clutter.
